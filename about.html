The company

Building an enduring AI and software company.

Zorn Novum Inc. is an early-stage company developing original AI models and practical software products. Our long-term focus is personal AI that people can shape around their own needs, with a straightforward experience and room to add capabilities over time.

Our direction

A useful foundation.
A long-term ambition.

We are pursuing that direction through focused development: an initial subscription application, substantial existing trading-platform work, and early research on our own neural-network models. These efforts are at different stages, and research progress is not the same as commercial readiness.

Our planned AI revenue model centers on one-time purchases of individual versions, followed by optional paid capabilities and future releases. Selected applications may use subscriptions. We aim to grow through useful products, careful testing and disciplined use of resources.

Founder & operating approach

Led by Keith Zorn.
Built with human direction.

Founder and CEO Keith Zorn leads the company’s strategy, product direction and development. He uses established AI tools under his direction and review, while retaining responsibility for decisions. That operating approach supports the company’s work today; our original models remain in development.

ZornHelix™

ZornHelix is the company’s AI-native operating platform for coordinating specialized AI roles and company workflows under human direction. It supports how Zorn Novum organizes work; it is separate from the personal AI product in development.

How we build

Evidence. Simplicity. User control.

Build from evidence.

Test what works, state limitations clearly and distinguish research progress from release readiness.

Keep the experience simple.

Start with a focused capability and make additional features optional.

Give users meaningful control.

Design for understandable teaching, reviewable changes and recovery to an earlier saved state.

Expand with discipline.

Develop and test supported components before adding new capabilities or broader uses.
