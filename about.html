Company

Zorn Novum is building a durable fintech company.

The company is focused on turning proprietary financial-market technology into practical software while protecting customer control, technical discipline, and the distinction between evidence and aspiration.

Current focus

Advance core market-intelligence technology and the customer product experience in parallel, while keeping broader infrastructure expansion subordinate to evidence and commercial priorities.

What we are building

Financial systems that move from intelligence to understandable action.

The core direction combines market discovery and evaluation with a customer experience designed for review and explicit authorization. The architecture is intended to keep the intelligence layer portable instead of tying the company's future to one execution environment.

Prototype development proceeds alongside evidence collection. Commercial claims strengthen only when product maturity, technical evidence, customer validation, and professional review support them.

How we operate

A restrained operating philosophy for consequential software.

The company favors clear boundaries, measurable evidence, and controlled expansion over hype or premature certainty.

Evidence first

Use evidence to earn stronger claims.

Internal progress is not automatically a public promise. Product maturity and consequential claims remain bounded by what can be supported.

User control

Keep final authorization visible.

The intended customer model keeps trading capital in customer-controlled accounts and preserves the customer's final decision over proposed actions.

Modular architecture

Design intelligence to remain portable.

Discovery and analysis are intended to remain separate from venue-specific execution so additional supported integrations can be evaluated over time.

Disciplined expansion

Do not build everything at once.

The company prioritizes core technology, the customer product, security, evidence, and commercialization readiness before broader adjacent infrastructure.

Commercial posture

Pre-commercial development with customer control at the center.

The intended business model is subscription-based and non-custodial: customers retain their own trading accounts and final authorization, while Zorn Novum develops the software and intelligence experience around them.

Exact commercial availability, pricing, supported platforms and jurisdictions, regulatory classification, disclosures, and related requirements are not presented as final here. Those areas require continued development and appropriate professional review before launch.
