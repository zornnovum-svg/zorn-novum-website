Company

Zorn Novum is building a durable fintech company.

We are developing financial-market technology around a practical goal: connect market intelligence with an understandable customer experience, while keeping consequential decisions in the customer’s hands.

Current focus

Core market intelligence and the customer platform are our immediate priorities. Broader infrastructure follows as evidence and commercial needs justify.

What we are building

A focused product. A long-term company.

The core direction combines market discovery and evaluation with a customer experience designed for review and explicit authorization. The architecture is intended to keep the intelligence layer portable instead of tying the company's future to one execution environment.

The intended subscription model centers on customers retaining their own trading accounts and final authorization. Prototype development, evidence collection, and customer validation shape the path toward commercial readiness.

Founder and purpose

Led by Keith Zorn.

Founder and CEO Keith Zorn has led Zorn Novum’s research, system architecture, development, integration, testing, and initial business planning.

He uses AI tools under his direction and review, while retaining responsibility for company decisions. His ambition is to build a trusted technology company that can endure beyond his own leadership.

The first commercial focus is the market-discovery application. Broader ideas remain long-term possibilities, with present effort centered on building a useful customer product.

How we operate

What guides our development.

The work is guided by four priorities: evidence, customer control, portable architecture, and focused development.

Evidence first

Test, learn, and improve.

Prototype development and evidence collection advance together, with customer validation and technical review guiding commercial readiness.

User control

Keep final authorization visible.

The intended customer model keeps trading capital in customer-controlled accounts and preserves the customer's final decision over proposed actions.

Modular architecture

Design intelligence to remain portable.

Discovery and analysis are intended to remain separate from venue-specific execution so additional supported integrations can be evaluated over time.

Disciplined expansion

Focus before expanding.

The company prioritizes core technology, the customer product, security, evidence, and commercialization readiness before broader adjacent infrastructure.

Commercial posture

A subscription model built around customer control.

The intended business model is subscription-based and non-custodial: customers retain their own trading accounts and final authorization, while Zorn Novum develops the software and intelligence experience around them.

The customer product remains in development. Pricing, supported platforms and jurisdictions, regulatory requirements, and launch availability depend on continued development and appropriate professional review.
