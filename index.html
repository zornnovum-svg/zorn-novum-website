Company

A focused company built around durable technology.

Zorn Novum is the company behind Z-Novum, its primary proprietary market-intelligence project. The current product direction is intentionally simple: separate market intelligence from broker-specific execution and keep customer assets in customer-controlled accounts.

The website is intentionally restrained while the product, evidence base, and commercialization path continue to mature.

Z-Novum

A clear boundary between intelligence and execution.

Z-Novum is being developed as a portable intelligence layer rather than a system defined by any single broker or exchange.

01

Broker-agnostic intelligence

Trade opportunities are intended to remain portable across supported execution environments rather than depend on one platform for discovery.

02

Customer-controlled custody

The intended model keeps customer trading capital in customer-controlled accounts rather than placing those assets in Zorn Novum custody.

03

Final customer authorization

Supported execution adapters are intended to help a customer review a proposed action while preserving the customer’s final authorization.

Principles

Evidence first. Claims second.

We are building around disciplined productization, clear customer control, and evidence that can stand on its own. Public claims should remain factual, supportable, and appropriately qualified.

Zorn Novum does not promise guaranteed trading results. Product capabilities, supported platforms, jurisdictions, and regulatory treatment remain subject to development and appropriate review before commercial launch.
