Zorn Novum / Z-Novum

AI-assisted market intelligence. Customer-defined control.

Zorn Novum is developing Z-Novum, a broker-agnostic market-intelligence platform designed to surface portable trade opportunities, apply them within user-defined parameters and account context, and preserve customer custody and final authorization.

Company thesis

Build intelligence that travels. Keep control with the customer.

Zorn Novum is building durable technology around a simple separation: market intelligence should not be defined by a single broker, and access to intelligence should not require a customer to surrender control of their assets or final decisions.

Z-Novum is the company’s primary proprietary technology project. The long-term objective is a portable intelligence layer that can connect to supported execution environments while keeping the underlying intelligence, customer account state, and execution responsibilities cleanly separated.

Evidence of execution

Progress is measured by what has actually been built, tested, and learned.

Zorn Novum is early-stage. The company’s public posture is to distinguish verified work from future plans and to keep unresolved questions visible rather than turn them into marketing claims.

Completed operating research

Live-market operating evidence

Z-Novum has been operated in a live real-money research environment with broker-reconciled state, contemporaneous decision records, and post-event review. The limited sample is not presented as proof of durable trading performance.

Established

Durable operating discipline

Versioned decision logic, externalized operating state, recovery and handoff procedures, independent review roles, and issue-to-control learning practices have been developed and exercised.

Approved direction

Account-aware product architecture

The current product direction separates shared market intelligence from customer-specific account context, settings, permissions, and risk application.

In development

User-authorized execution proof

The first customer-facing prototype is intentionally narrow: qualified opportunity, customer review, an explicit Execute or Leave decision, validated backend handling, and broker confirmation.

Current stage

Private development and controlled prototype work

Current priorities include account-aware product architecture, secure handling of authorized account context, controlled execution validation, and a minimal customer prototype. No public commercial launch date is being claimed.

Why the architecture matters

AI can assist without becoming the owner of the customer relationship.

01

Portable intelligence

Opportunity discovery is intended to remain independent of any single broker so the core intelligence can evolve separately from execution adapters.

02

Protected customer state

Account-specific information and permissions are intended to stay within controlled backend logic rather than become part of the public client or model prompt.

03

Replaceable AI components

AI is treated as a reasoning component, not the durable owner of product state, credentials, or the company’s proprietary operating knowledge.

04

Human authority

The current approved product direction preserves customer review and final authorization rather than defaulting to black-box autonomous execution.

Disciplined development

Unknowns stay unknown until the evidence changes.

Large-sample performance durability, capital capacity, commercial demand, production software maturity, security hardening, and legal or regulatory treatment all require additional evidence and professional review.

That uncertainty is not hidden. The objective is to keep building the evidence, product controls, and technical infrastructure needed to answer those questions responsibly.

What remains protected

Transparency about progress does not require disclosure of proprietary IP.

The public site can explain the product model, development stage, control philosophy, and evidence discipline without publishing the proprietary engine itself.

Algorithm logic, formulas, thresholds, prompts, internal scoring, source mappings, credentials, account details, internal tickets, and sensitive implementation details remain outside the public disclosure boundary.

Principles

Evidence first. Claims second.

Zorn Novum is building around disciplined productization, clear customer control, portability, and evidence that can stand on its own.

Zorn Novum does not promise guaranteed trading results. Product capabilities, supported platforms, jurisdictions, security posture, and regulatory treatment remain subject to development, testing, and appropriate professional review before commercial launch.
