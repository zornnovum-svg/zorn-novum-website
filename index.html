The company

Building an AI technology company with a focused direction.

Zorn Novum's long-term product is a proprietary business AI platform, envisioned as a technical partner that can assess workflows and help implement practical business improvements. Today that is a research and engineering goal, not an operating enterprise service.

Selective custom software and integration projects provide commercial opportunities and real-world development experience. GreenToSpend is approaching an initial release, while Zorn Lumen development is paused. Our central development priority remains the business AI platform.

Proposed future business AI experience

From understanding a business to improving it.

The intended platform has two possible starting points: a business can request a defined system, or optionally ask for a broader operational assessment. The following are planned capabilities, not services available today.

A customer could begin with a specific software or infrastructure requirement, without a company-wide assessment.

An optional assessment could examine approved business workflows and, with appropriate consent and privacy safeguards, gather staff perspectives. These controls still require development and testing.

The goal is to identify practical changes in processes, organization and technical systems, with recommendations reviewed by the customer.

Over time, the AI is intended to help create, connect and maintain the software and infrastructure required to implement approved improvements. Reliability and scope remain to be demonstrated.

Early-stage R&D: the proprietary platform cannot yet perform this full workflow for customers.
