The personal AI experience / product goals

Start simply. Shape it over time.

The intended first release focuses on text interaction, guided setup and a manageable teaching process.

The first intended release would provide a focused text experience on your own computer, with guided setup. Supported hardware remains to be established.

We are working toward an understandable process for teaching a personal AI over time. Reliable customer learning remains to be established.

Planned controls would let you review proposed learning changes, accept them or return to an earlier saved state.

Later development may add internet research, specialized models and optional plugins, with tested, compatible combinations.

These are planned capabilities. A consumer-ready AI product is not currently available.
