The discovery problem

A watchlist starts with what you already chose to watch.

That works when the opportunity is already on your radar. It is much less useful when the first problem is determining what deserves attention in the first place.

Z-Novum is intended to help determine what deserves attention.

Two ways to use Z-Novum

Use Z-Novum's intelligence—or define the analysis yourself.

Customers should not have to adopt one proprietary methodology to benefit from broader-market discovery.

Mode 01 Proprietary

Z-Novum Intelligence

Z-Novum's proprietary market-discovery mode. It is being designed to scan supported markets for opportunities identified by Z-Novum's intelligence while applying supported customer-selected parameters, account context, risk boundaries, and controls.

The intelligence itself remains protected. Results can vary with configuration, market conditions, fees, execution, supported markets, risk settings, and other conditions.

Mode 02 In development

Custom Market Scan

Define the analytical conditions you want to find, then search the supported market universe for matches rather than checking only a predetermined watchlist.

The value is search, filtering, organization, and explanation—not a promise of profitable outcomes.

Custom Market Scan

Your criteria. A broader field to search.

Custom Market Scan is intended for traders who already know what characteristics they want to find. Instead of manually checking a small list of instruments, the system is intended to apply supported analytical criteria across a broader supported universe and organize the matches.

Conceptual / in development

“Find liquid crypto assets showing rising volume, positive short-term momentum, and volatility inside my selected range.”

Natural-language interpretation is being developed as a way to translate ordinary requests into explicit supported analytical criteria. The goal is not hidden inference: customers should be able to understand what criteria were actually applied and why a result matched.

Z-Novum Intelligence is the proprietary mode. The public product story is intentionally about what it is designed to do for the customer—not the formulas, scoring logic, thresholds, prompts, model controls, or source mappings that make the intelligence proprietary.

Discovery to decision

Finding something is only the beginning.

The intended workflow carries an opportunity from market data through explanation and customer review without treating execution as automatic.

01

Supported market data

The searchable market universe begins with supported market information.

02

Intelligence or criteria

Use Z-Novum Intelligence or customer-defined analytical criteria.

03

Context

Apply relevant customer, account, risk, and control context.

04

Matching opportunity

Surface something that fits the applicable conditions.

05

Explanation

Show why the opportunity matched rather than returning an unexplained result.

06

Customer review

Put the opportunity in front of the customer for an explicit decision.

Customer decision Execute

Authorize an action through a supported execution adapter when applicable.

Customer decision Leave

Decline the opportunity and take no action. No action is a valid outcome.

Whole-market discovery

Start broad. Narrow with purpose.

The core visual idea behind Z-Novum is a large field of instruments progressively reduced to a small set that fits the active intelligence or analytical conditions.

Development status

Current evidence, active development, and future direction stay separate.

Zorn Novum does not treat a planned capability as a current capability simply because it appears on a roadmap.

01 Current / verified

Active proprietary technology project

Z-Novum is Zorn Novum's primary proprietary technology and market-intelligence project. Development is active, and the public site deliberately distinguishes verified facts from claims that still require evidence.

02 In development

Customer-facing discovery and control

Broader-market discovery, Custom Market Scan, natural-language criteria interpretation, customer/account-aware workflows, explanation, review, Execute or Leave, and execution-adapter handling remain under development or validation.

03 Planned / future direction

Expansion beyond the initial environment

Additional execution venues, markets, asset classes, integrations, and professional capabilities remain future direction subject to technical evidence, commercial strategy, security work, and legal or regulatory review.

What remains unproven

Unknowns stay visible until evidence changes them.

Large-sample performance durability, commercial demand, production software maturity, security hardening, regulatory treatment, and broader multi-platform expansion all require additional evidence, testing, or professional review. The site will be updated as those facts become supportable.

Architecture and portability

The intelligence should travel farther than one execution venue.

Kraken is an initial execution-environment and adapter direction—not the permanent boundary of Z-Novum's intelligence.

The architectural goal is to keep discovery and analysis separate from venue-specific execution so the intelligence layer can remain portable as the platform develops.

Evidence discipline

Evidence first. Claims second.

Zorn Novum is building the public story around what can actually be supported. Performance, product maturity, supported venues, security posture, commercial traction, and other consequential claims should become stronger only when the underlying evidence becomes stronger.

Proprietary algorithms, formulas, scoring systems, thresholds, prompts, source mappings, credentials, customer/account data, internal tickets, and sensitive security implementation remain outside the public disclosure boundary.
