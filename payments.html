For approved projects

How payment works.

  1. Review your proposal.

    Confirm the scope, price, deliverables, payment timing, review process, usage limits and support arrangements before accepting.

  2. Open your invoice.

    We send your project-specific invoice with the amount, due date, payment schedule and available payment methods. Larger projects may receive an invoice for each agreed stage.

  3. Pay using the invoice.

    Use the secure link in your invoice to pay with an available method, such as ACH bank transfer, wire or card. Card and wallet options appear when enabled for that invoice.

Work begins after payment.

Graphics packages and defined deliverables are paid in full before work starts. Larger projects are paid in agreed stages before each stage begins. Monthly hosting, support and graphics services are billed in advance according to the proposal.

Payment methods

ACH without an added fee.

ACH bank transfers and wires have no added Zorn Novum payment fee. If card payment is offered for an invoice, an additional fee may apply to eligible credit-card transactions only. The exact fee will be disclosed before payment and shown on the invoice or receipt.

No surcharge is applied to debit or prepaid cards. Any card surcharge is subject to applicable law, card-network rules and processor requirements. Available payment methods are shown on each invoice.

Hosting and usage

Know the monthly cost before it starts.

For hosted software, Zorn Novum estimates the required service and expected usage as the application is built. The proposal identifies the monthly fee, included resources and support, and explains how any measured or additional usage will be calculated.

We agree recurring charges and usage limits with you before billing starts. Any third-party AI, paid API, domain or other project-specific charge is identified in the proposal or capped as agreed.

Invoice questions

Need help with an invoice?

Email orders@zornnovum.com. Include your invoice number if you have it. Please do not email card numbers, bank credentials, passwords or sensitive files.
