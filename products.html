Early development

Personal AI models

We are developing original neural-network models with a simple product goal: let people run a personal AI on their own computer and teach it without needing to become AI engineers.

The first intended release focuses on text interaction, guided setup and a manageable teaching process. Planned controls would let users review proposed learning changes, accept them or return to an earlier saved state.

Later development may add internet research, specialized models and optional plugins. We intend to offer tested, compatible combinations so customers can choose capabilities that fit their needs.

Buy a version. Keep using it.

The planned commercial model is a one-time purchase for continued use of a particular version, with optional paid additions and future versions. Pricing, license terms, support periods and computer requirements will be defined before release. Online features may involve separate service costs.

These are product goals under development. A consumer-ready AI product is not currently available.

In development

Everyday money-planning application

Our near-term commercial priority is completing a focused application to help people organize everyday money planning. A subscription model is planned for this product. Its public name, pricing and release details will be announced when ready.

Substantial development work completed; further work remains

Trading-platform technology

Zorn Novum has completed substantial development work on a trading platform. That work provides experience in market discovery, decision workflows, testing and software integration. Further validation and release work remain.

ZornQuanta™

The financial market intelligence system behind this work, focused on discovering and evaluating potential opportunities.

ZornLumen™

The customer-facing platform being developed to bring that intelligence into an understandable discovery and decision experience, with final control remaining with the user.

The company’s original personal AI models remain in development and are not presented as powering this platform.

Longer-term direction

Tailored business software

As our AI capabilities develop, we intend to explore software built around a company’s particular workflows and requirements. That longer-term ambition involving our own AI remains in development. Separately, we offer focused custom software services for agreed business needs today; those projects do not rely on our original AI models.

Common questions

A clearer picture of what’s ahead.

Can I buy the personal AI today?

Not yet. Model research and product development are underway.

Will the AI require a subscription?

Our planned core model is a one-time purchase for continued use of the purchased version. Optional additions, newer versions and connected services may have separate costs.

Will I be able to teach it myself?

That is a central product goal. We are working toward an understandable teaching process with review and recovery controls. Reliable customer learning has not yet been established.

Will every feature work offline?

The initial core is intended to run locally. Future internet-enabled features would require connectivity. Supported hardware and each release’s limitations will be specified before sale.
