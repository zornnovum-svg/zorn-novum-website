AI and technology

Business AI first.
Software that proves execution.

Our priority is a proprietary business AI platform. Existing software and technical assets support engineering experience and selective commercial opportunities; each remains at its own stage of readiness.

Primary R&D / not commercially available

Proprietary business AI platform

Zorn Novum is working toward AI that can understand a company's operations, evaluate business challenges and help implement customer-approved improvements through software, integrations and technical infrastructure. The intended product is a business technology platform, not merely a code-writing assistant.

Two possible starting points

Customers could ask for a defined system or integration. Separately, an optional broader assessment may examine approved workflows and seek staff input under suitable authorization, consent and privacy protections. Both approaches remain planned platform capabilities.

From recommendations to implementation

The long-term goal is to help businesses plan, build, connect, operate and improve their systems. A subscription-based AI offering and deployment options are contemplated; final prices, security requirements and product boundaries are still under development.

An early AI prototype exists locally, but it does not yet demonstrate the full platform workflow, autonomous enterprise implementation or customer-ready reliability.

Functional / pre-release

GreenToSpend

GreenToSpend is a functional Android financial-planning application developed by Zorn Novum. It includes supporting lightweight subscription and account-linked licensing infrastructure. Final refinements, release graphics and distribution steps are in progress.

Its intended launch uses a modest marketing effort to learn from product operations and customer response. GreenToSpend is a secondary commercial product, not the company's primary AI development or funding focus. No market adoption or revenue outcome is being claimed.

Development paused / research preserved

Zorn Lumen and market research

Zorn Novum has completed prior development and testing work on trading-platform technology, including ZornQuanta research and Zorn Lumen application components. Active platform development is now on hold while resources concentrate on business AI. No public trading product or performance claim is being made.

Existing API integration and MCP connector work is preserved. These components are currently tailored to Kraken and would need further adaptation, validation, security review and appropriate rights before any separate commercial use.

Available by project agreement

Custom business software and integrations

Zorn Novum selectively develops software, applications, automation, API integrations and supporting infrastructure for businesses today. Where needed, projects can use approved third-party AI services; they are not powered by our proprietary business AI in development. Explore current services.

We also maintain a research library of possible future software opportunities. These are subject to reassessment and customer validation, not commitments to launch additional products.

Common questions

A clearer picture of what’s ahead.

Can businesses use Zorn Novum's proprietary AI today?

Not yet. Early model experiments are underway, but the planned assessment, implementation and ongoing AI platform functions are not commercially available.

How would a business begin with the planned AI?

The two intended paths are a defined build or integration request and an optional broader operational assessment. Their detailed capabilities and privacy controls still need development and validation.

Will the AI be subscription based?

The current business plan contemplates an AI subscription, with options for deployment, hosting and separately purchased systems. Final terms and pricing have not been approved.

What can Zorn Novum provide today?

Selected custom software, applications, integrations and support projects are considered under individual agreements. They use current development methods and may use separately approved third-party AI services.
