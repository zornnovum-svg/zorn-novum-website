Market Discovery & Decision Platform

A single-account prototype that connects market discovery, account information, order preparation, and customer-approved submission through Kraken. The product remains under development.

In development

Your account. Your decision.

The intended model is non-custodial: customer trading capital remains in customer-controlled accounts, and the customer retains final authorization over whether a proposed action is submitted.

The problem

Intelligence is only useful when the decision path is understandable.

A market opportunity can lose value if the customer cannot quickly understand what is being presented, why it matters, and what action is actually being requested. A customer product needs to connect discovery and decision without hiding the consequential step.

Intended value

Bring account information, trade review, and authorization into one app.

The product is being designed to help self-directed traders look beyond familiar watchlists, understand why opportunities surfaced, and move through a connected review and decision process. Reducing manual searching and switching between tools is a goal to test with customers.

The prototype brings trade review, account information, and explicitly approved execution into the application while keeping exchange API credentials on the server. The customer remains the decision-maker; recommendations do not automatically become trades.

Prototype workflow

Review the trade. Approve the submission.

Start from a notification’s Trade button, which selects the relevant asset when available, or enter a trade manually. You choose the amount and order details.

01

Choose a market

Browse available Kraken markets and request current prices through the server connection.

02

Prepare

Choose a supported order type and amount. Order checks cover market minimums, precision, available funds, and fees.

03

Review

Check the order details, calculated quantity, and estimated funding requirements before proceeding.

04

Approve

Explicitly authorize submission through the server connection. Recommendations and notifications never authorize a trade on their own.

Account information and submission results.

The account view shows available USD separately from nonzero asset holdings, followed by active orders. The last account snapshot remains visible between updates.

Submission results distinguish accepted, pending, rejected, and uncertain states. Persistent recovery records let the app check an interrupted submission and help prevent duplicate orders.

Customer control

Final authorization stays with the customer.

The intended architecture separates market intelligence from platform-specific execution. That keeps the intelligence portable while allowing execution connections to be added or evaluated independently.

Exchange API credentials stay on the server. The phone client signs requests with a private key that remains on the device.

Development update / October 2026

Customer-approved execution in a single-account prototype.

The prototype supports market, limit, stop-loss, and take-profit orders through Kraken, subject to market rules and supported amount units. Supported entry orders can include one attached stop-loss or take-profit exit.

Expanded USD sizing has been implemented but awaits deployment and app integration. Other exchanges are planned.

Commercial release still requires further engineering, integration work, security, reliability, customer validation, and appropriate legal or regulatory review. Customer benefits and commercial readiness remain to be established.

Limitations

Availability and important considerations.

  • The current product is a single-account prototype under development.
  • Combined take-profit/stop-loss management and automatic cancellation of the remaining exit are not implemented.
  • No launch date, price, free-trial structure, or customer eligibility is promised on this page.
  • No specific broker, exchange, market, asset class, or jurisdiction is promised as commercially supported.
  • The legal and regulatory treatment of the exact product flow must be determined by qualified professionals before commercial launch.
  • No product design choice, authorization step, or disclaimer should be interpreted as eliminating applicable regulatory obligations.
