Market Discovery & Decision Platform

A customer-facing product direction intended to carry market intelligence into a clear discovery, review, decision, and supported-action workflow while keeping final authorization with the customer.

In development

Customer-control principle

The intended model is non-custodial: customer trading capital remains in customer-controlled accounts, and the customer retains final authorization over whether a proposed action is submitted.

The problem

Intelligence is only useful when the decision path is understandable.

A market opportunity can lose value if the customer cannot quickly understand what is being presented, why it matters, and what action is actually being requested. A customer product needs to connect discovery and decision without hiding the consequential step.

Intended value

Bring discovery, context, and authorization into one product flow.

The platform is intended to present structured market opportunities to subscribed users, provide relevant context for review, and connect to supported execution environments where appropriate.

The customer remains the decision-maker. The product direction is not based on Zorn Novum taking custody of customer trading capital or silently executing every identified opportunity.

High-level operation

Discovery → review → decision → supported action.

01

Discover

Receive structured intelligence from the company's market-analysis technology.

02

Review

See the opportunity and relevant context in a customer-facing experience.

03

Decide

Choose whether to act. Declining or taking no action remains a valid outcome.

04

Authorize

When supported, explicitly authorize submission through the selected connected platform.

Customer control

Final authorization stays with the customer.

The intended architecture separates market intelligence from platform-specific execution. That keeps the intelligence portable while allowing execution connections to be added or evaluated independently.

Where technically feasible, the product direction also favors minimum necessary permissions and avoids withdrawal authority over customer assets.

Current status

The customer product is being built and validated.

Prototype work can proceed while the underlying evidence base continues to mature. Early device, notification, formatting, reliability, or usability testing should be understood as technical or product testing, not proof of a finished commercial application or broad market validation.

Commercial launch requires additional product engineering, security, reliability, customer validation, operational readiness, and appropriate legal or regulatory review.

Limitations

Important boundaries remain unresolved.

  • No launch date, price, free-trial structure, or customer eligibility is promised on this page.
  • No specific broker, exchange, market, asset class, or jurisdiction is promised as commercially supported.
  • The legal and regulatory treatment of the exact product flow must be determined by qualified professionals before commercial launch.
  • No product design choice, authorization step, or disclaimer should be interpreted as eliminating applicable regulatory obligations.
In development means in development.

The intended workflow describes product direction. It is not a representation that every element is production-ready or commercially available today.
