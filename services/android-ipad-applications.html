What the proposal defines

Know what will be delivered.

  • The screens, navigation and functions specified for the agreed Android or iPad target.
  • A written definition of supported devices and operating-system versions, with the agreed testing and review criteria.
  • The delivery or installation method, distribution responsibilities, handover information and support arrangements listed in the proposal.

Deliverables, included revisions, acceptance criteria, third-party costs and recurring charges are specified in your proposal. Usage rights, ownership, licensing and any reuse or exclusivity arrangements are explained in the agreement.

Scope boundaries

Support for all Android devices, every iPad version or iPhone is not assumed. Multiple platforms, offline behavior, account systems, device features and connections to other software require individual assessment. App-store submission or another distribution route must be agreed; store approval and review timing are not guaranteed. Hosting and back-end services are included only where specified.

Before you inquire

Common questions

Can one project cover Android and iPad?

We can assess a project that needs both. Each target adds requirements for implementation, testing and distribution. The $4,000 starting point applies to one agreed Android or iPad target, not a promise of both platforms.

Will this be a browser tool or an installed app?

That depends on the need. We consider the workflow, devices, access requirements and delivery constraints before specifying the format in the proposal.

Does iPad support include iPhone?

No. The current offering names Android and iPad. Device support and operating-system versions are expressly defined for each project; iPhone support is not implied.

What happens when devices or operating systems change?

Ongoing maintenance and later compatibility work follow the support arrangements in the agreement. They are not an unlimited part of the initial development price.

Tell us what you want to improve.

Describe the need, how you handle it today and who would use the result. Please do not include passwords, credentials, sensitive files or private customer records.
