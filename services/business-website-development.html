Your business need

Make your offer easier to understand.

A small business may need one well-defined page before it needs a larger website. Another may already have useful content but make visitors work too hard to find services or send a request. We begin with the visitor's task, the content you have and the change you want to make.

If you need a website for a new business, a focused first page can explain your services and give prospective customers a clear way to inquire. Our website development services cover agreed business webpages, inquiry forms, and improvements, with additional pages and features scoped separately.

What the proposal defines

Know what will be delivered.

  • The pages, sections or changes listed in the proposal, with agreed content and supplied brand assets.
  • For form work: the field list, submission destination, visitor instructions and agreed delivery checks.
  • Review of the agreed desktop and mobile layouts, links and navigation, plus the specified handover or publishing arrangements.

Deliverables, included revisions, acceptance criteria, third-party costs and recurring charges are specified in your proposal. Usage rights, ownership, licensing and any reuse or exclusivity arrangements are explained in the agreement.

Scope boundaries

A focused webpage is not a commitment to an unrestricted website rebuild. Additional pages, account systems, commerce, integrations and complex applications require individual assessment and a separate scope where appropriate. Existing-site work also depends on the platform and authorized access available. Content, revisions, hosting and ongoing upkeep are included only as specified in the proposal.

Before you inquire

Common questions

Can you improve the website I already have?

We can assess a defined improvement to an existing website. Share the public website address and the change you need; platform constraints and access requirements are reviewed before acceptance. Do not send passwords with your initial request.

Will a form actually deliver inquiries?

The project must specify a working submission destination and how delivery will be checked. A form's appearance alone does not establish delivery. Any third-party submission service and its costs are agreed first.

Does the starting price include hosting and ongoing edits?

The starting price covers the agreed development scope. Hosting, upkeep, additional pages and later changes are included only if the proposal expressly lists them. See our hosting and support service for current recurring-price guidance.

Tell us what you want to improve.

Describe the need, how you handle it today and who would use the result. Please do not include passwords, credentials, sensitive files or private customer records.
