Your business need

Start with the work you want to improve.

Tell us where information is entered, what decisions follow and what the output should be. A calculator needs approved formulas; a tracker needs clear stages; an inventory tool needs agreed records and update rules. Establishing those details gives both sides a concrete scope to review.

For a new business, custom software can start with one defined task: a calculator using your approved rules, a request tracker, or a dashboard based on an agreed spreadsheet format. We assess the requirements and agree the scope before development begins.

What the proposal defines

Know what will be delivered.

  • The agreed screens, inputs, calculations, workflow states and outputs for the defined function.
  • Specified data formats and representative, non-sensitive test examples, with review and acceptance criteria.
  • The agreed delivery format, supported devices and versions, handover details, and any separately scoped hosting or support.

Deliverables, included revisions, acceptance criteria, third-party costs and recurring charges are specified in your proposal. Usage rights, ownership, licensing and any reuse or exclusivity arrangements are explained in the agreement.

Scope boundaries

Each application is assessed individually. Inventory, quoting and tracking examples do not imply a comprehensive enterprise system or every possible feature. Multi-user access, migration, integrations and more complex applications can change the scope substantially. We consider Windows computers, Android devices and iPad; browser-based or installable delivery depends on the project. Support for every device is not assumed.

Before you inquire

Common questions

Can the tool use my existing spreadsheet?

Potentially. For dashboards and reports we agree the spreadsheet or CSV format, fields and calculations before work begins. Importing, cleaning or migrating a larger set of records requires assessment and is not automatically included.

Who approves the calculations?

You provide and approve the formulas and assumptions used for the agreed tool. The scope should identify inputs, expected outputs and examples for review, including how missing or invalid inputs are handled.

Does your original AI power this software?

No. Our original AI models remain in development and do not power these customer projects. If a project needs an AI-assisted integration, we assess the use of agreed third-party services separately.

Will I own everything exclusively?

Usage rights, ownership, licensing and any reuse or exclusivity arrangements are explained in the proposal and agreement. Editable or source files and third-party components are addressed for the particular project.

Tell us what you want to improve.

Describe the need, how you handle it today and who would use the result. Please do not include passwords, credentials, sensitive files or private customer records.
